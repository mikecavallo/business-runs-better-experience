Questions people ask

Will it make things up?

It's set up to answer only from your documents, show its sources, and say it doesn't know when the answer isn't there. Nothing is perfect, so important answers stay easy to check.

Is our data safe?

Your documents stay in accounts you control. I use business AI services that don't train on your data, or a private model on your own hardware if you need more.

What do we need to have ready?

Whatever you already have: price lists, manuals, shared drives, past emails. Messy is fine. Organizing it is part of the job.

Can it connect to our software?

Often, yes. It can look things up in your customer list (CRM), job system or shared drive, so answers include live information.
