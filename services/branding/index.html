Questions people ask

Do I need a full rebrand?

Not always. Sometimes a cleaner logo and consistent colors are enough. I'll tell you honestly what you need.

What files will I get?

Your logo in vector and web formats (SVG, PNG and PDF), color codes, fonts, and a brand guide that explains how to use them.

Can you work with my existing logo?

Yes. If you like your logo, I can build a consistent identity around it instead of starting over.

How much does branding cost?

Every project gets a fixed price before work starts, based on what you need: a logo refresh costs far less than a full identity with templates.
