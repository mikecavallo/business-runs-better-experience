Questions people ask

Are local models as good as ChatGPT?

For many everyday jobs, like summarizing, drafting, searching documents and transcription, yes. For the hardest reasoning tasks the biggest cloud models are still ahead. I'll be straight with you about the trade-off for your use.

What hardware do I need?

It depends on the model and how many people use it. Many small teams can start with one well-equipped workstation with a modern graphics card. You get a specific recommendation before you buy anything.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once it’s set up, it runs without an internet connection.
