Yes. I can set up your account and listings, or take over the parts that eat your time. Amazon has strict rules, and I've learned most of them the hard way.

Yes. I move products, customers and order history, and set up redirects so your search rankings come with you.

Can my inventory sync between my store and marketplaces?

Yes. It's one of the first things I set up, because overselling costs you money and ratings.
