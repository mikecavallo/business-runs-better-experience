Questions people ask

What can be automated?

Anything repetitive with clear rules: moving data between apps, sending reminders, creating documents, routing requests and building reports. If a person does it the same way every time, it's a candidate.

What tools do you use?

Mostly n8n (an automation platform), Python and the built-in connections of the tools you already use, like Google Workspace, Stripe, Airtable or your CRM. I pick what fits, not what's trendy.

What happens when something breaks?

Every build includes 30 days of fixes. After that, monthly support starts at $300 a month and includes monitoring, so problems get fixed before you notice them.

Will my team need to learn new software?

As little as possible. Most automations run in the background of the tools your team already uses.

How much does it cost?

Builds start at $2,500 per system, at a fixed price agreed before work starts. Half to start, half when it’s live and your team is trained.
