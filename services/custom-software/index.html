Questions people ask

Why not just use off-the-shelf software?

Often you should, and I'll tell you when. Custom software makes sense when you've outgrown your tools, pay for features you don't use, or glue five apps together by hand.

Can you connect the tools we already use?

Usually, yes. Most modern software is built to connect to other tools, and I've connected CRMs, calendars, forms, payment systems and online marketplaces.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Accounts are set up in your business’s name, and you get the logins and documentation at handoff.

What happens if something breaks?

Every build includes 30 days of fixes. After that, ongoing support starts at $300 a month and includes monitoring, so problems get fixed before you notice them.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the size of the job, so every project gets a fixed quote first. Smaller tools and integrations start from $2,500.

Can you add AI to software we already have?

Often, yes. Summaries, smart search, drafted replies and sorting incoming requests can usually be added without rebuilding everything.

Got a process held together with spreadsheets?

Tell me about it. I reply within one business day with what I'd build, roughly what it involves, and whether something off the shelf would do.
