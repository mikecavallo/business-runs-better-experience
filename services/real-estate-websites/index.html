Questions agents ask

What is IDX?

IDX (Internet Data Exchange) is the system that lets MLS listings show on your own website. Buyers search homes on your site, and the leads come to you instead of a listing portal.

Can you work with my MLS?

IDX access is approved MLS by MLS. I work with your board and your IDX provider on the paperwork and make sure the site follows their display rules.

I already have an IDX provider. Can you use it?

Usually, yes. I can build a new site around your current provider, or help you move to a better one if it's holding you back.

Do you build brokerage sites with agent pages?

Yes. A brokerage site with a page for every agent, their listings and their contact details is what I run every day.

What does a real estate website cost?

Every real estate site gets a fixed quote before work starts. IDX feed fees from your MLS or provider are separate, and you'll know what they are up front.
