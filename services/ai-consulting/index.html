Questions people ask

Is AI worth it for a small business?

Often, for specific jobs: answering calls, following up on leads, drafting quotes and answering repeat questions. Rarely as a vague “AI strategy”. The audit finds out which jobs those are for you.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I handle the technical side and train your team on the parts they use day to day. If you can use email and a calendar, you can use what I build.

Do I have to buy a build after the audit?

No. The plan is yours to keep and use however you like. If you build with me within 60 days, the $500 is credited in full.

Is my business data safe?

I only access what a project needs, use your accounts rather than mine wherever possible, and don't use your data for anything else. For sensitive data there are business AI plans that don't train on it, and private models that run on your own hardware.

What if I've already tried ChatGPT?

Good, that helps. Most businesses get far more out of AI once it’s connected to their own tools and data, not just used in a chat window.
