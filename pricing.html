Frequently asked

What do I get for $499?

A five-page website with a custom design, built mobile-first and set up for local search, with a contact form that reaches you. Extra pages, online stores, booking systems and logins are quoted separately.

Why do SEO and ads say “from”?

Because the work grows with your market. A business in one town with a few competitors needs less than one competing across the whole Cape Fear region. You get the exact monthly price in writing before we start, and it doesn't change unless the scope does.

Is my ad budget included?

No. Your ad budget is paid straight to Google or Meta from your own account, so you see every dollar spent. My fee covers setting up, running and reporting on the campaigns, with no markup on your spend.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I handle the technical side and train your team on the parts they use day to day. If you can use email and a calendar, you can use what I build.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local and service businesses: contractors, home services, real estate, clinics, shops, agencies and similar. Most are run by an owner or a small team who need a better website, more customers or more hours in the week.

What does “from $2,500” depend on?

How many tools it has to connect to, how much custom logic it needs, and how many people will use it. You get a fixed price before anything starts, and the price doesn't change unless the scope does.

What if the audit finds AI isn't worth it for my business yet?

Then the plan says so, and tells you what to fix first. Plenty of the best wins are simple automations that don't need AI at all.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Accounts are set up in your business's name, and you get the logins and documentation at handoff.

Is my business data safe?

I only access what a project needs, use your accounts rather than mine wherever possible, and don’t use your data for anything else. See the privacy policy.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Most work happens over video calls and screen shares, and local businesses can meet in person.

Tell me what you need

A new website, more customers or hours back in your week. Tell me what you have in mind and I reply within one business day with a practical next step.
