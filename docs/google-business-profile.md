# Google Business Profile: setup sheet

Copy-paste values for setting up Business Runs Better on Google (Search and Maps).
Start at <https://business.google.com/create>.

## Eligibility, read first

Google only allows a profile for businesses that **meet customers in person**, either at an
address or by traveling to them. Online-only businesses are not eligible and get suspended.
In-person Time Audits at the client's business qualify, so set it up as a **service-area
business**, actually offer in-person visits, and hide your home address.

| Field | Value |

| Phone | A number you'll answer. A free Google Voice number keeps your cell private. |
| Website | `https://businessrunsbetter.com` |
| Appointment link | Your booking link (Cal.com / Odoo) once it's live, otherwise `https://businessrunsbetter.com/pricing.html#contact` |
| Hours | Your real availability, e.g. Mon–Fri 9 AM–5 PM |
| Opening date | The month you started Business Runs Better |

Verification: Google usually asks for a short continuous **video**: show your street sign or
surroundings, your work setup, and something that proves you run the business (logo on a
laptop, business cards, a client invoice). Have that ready before you start.

## Business description (750 characters max; this one is 676)

Google's guidelines say the description can't focus on prices, promotions or sales, so prices go in
Services below. Leave the website sale out of Google entirely (list $499, not "was $899").

```
Business Runs Better builds websites, brings in customers and puts AI to work for local
businesses in Leland, Wilmington and the Cape Fear region. I'm Mike Cavallo, a developer with
more than 20 years of experience, certified by IDX Broker for real estate websites. I build
fast, mobile-first websites, landing pages, real estate sites with live listings and online
stores. I run local SEO and Google and Facebook ads, tracked back to real leads. And I build
AI phone receptionists, automatic lead follow-up and custom tools that connect the software
you already use. Every job gets a fixed price before work starts, and you deal with one person
from the first call to launch.
```

## Photos (add at least these)

1. **Logo:** square, from `assets/img/social.webp` or the site's logo mark.
2. **Cover:** a wide brand image (`assets/img/hero.webp` works).
3. **Headshot:** `assets/img/mike-cavallo.webp`. Profiles with a real face get more clicks.
4. **At work:** a few real photos of you working with a client or at your desk. Add more over time.

## After it's live

- **Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor.** Ask every happy client. Get your review link from
  the profile (Ask for reviews) and add it to your follow-up emails.
- Reply to every review, good or bad.
- Post an update every week or two (a quick tip, a finished project, a before/after).
- Keep the name, phone and website identical everywhere (website, Facebook, Yelp, LinkedIn).
  Mismatches hurt local ranking.
